Who We Are

A Legacy on
Scottish Waters

Edinburgh University Boat Club is one of Scotland's oldest and most celebrated rowing clubs. We bring together students of all abilities — from absolute beginners to competitive athletes — united by a passion for the sport and the camaraderie that comes with it.

Our squads compete at regional, national, and international level, representing the University of Edinburgh with pride.

What We Do

Life on the Water

🚣
Sweep Rowing

Compete in fours and eights at regattas across the UK, including BUCS and Scottish Rowing Championships.

From local head races to national regattas, our crews race throughout the year at every level of the sport.

Get Involved

Ready to Row?

Whether you have never sat in a boat or have rowed for years, EUBC welcomes you. Join us at the start of term and discover one of Edinburgh's most rewarding student experiences. No experience necessary — just turn up and we'll take care of the rest.
